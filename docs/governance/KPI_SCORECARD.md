# Root-to-Leaf KPI Scorecard & Monthly Governance (CON-674)

## 1. Objective
Establish a **Root-to-Leaf KPI scorecard** and a lightweight monthly governance rhythm to track strategic alignment, delivery health, and risk posture.

## 2. KPI Scorecard (Initial Baseline)

| KPI | Definition | Source / owner | Baseline (set this month) | Target (90 days) | Reporting cadence |
| -- | -- | -- | -- | -- | -- |
| Strategic objective coverage | % of approved root objectives mapped to active leaf initiatives/issues | Strategy + PMO (@botshelomokoka) | 85% | >= 90% | Monthly |
| Phase 5/6 milestone on-time rate | % of planned milestones delivered by committed date | Engineering + Delivery | 70% | >= 85% | Monthly |
| Dependency risk closure rate | % of identified cross-team dependencies resolved within SLA | Eng leads + Governance | 75% | >= 80% | Monthly |
| Security/compliance readiness | % of required controls evidencing pass status (MVCR/compliance gates) | Security + Compliance | 90% | 100% critical green | Monthly |
| UX simplification progress | % completion of approved token UX rationalization workstream | Product + UX | 20% | >= 75% | Monthly |
| Production incident stability | Change in Sev1/Sev2 incidents related to Phase 5/6 scope | SRE + Engineering | 0 | -30% from baseline | Monthly |

## 3. Monthly Governance Cadence Checklist

- [ ] Week 1: Refresh KPI values from source systems and verify data quality.
- [ ] Week 1: Review variance vs target and identify top 3 drivers.
- [ ] Week 2: Run Root-to-Leaf governance review with engineering/product/security owners.
- [ ] Week 2: Record decisions, owners, and due dates for corrective actions.
- [ ] Week 3: Update linked delivery issues/epics with approved action items.
- [ ] Week 4: Publish monthly scorecard snapshot and risk/decision summary.
- [ ] Week 4: Confirm carry-over actions and next-month priorities.

## 4. Deliverables

- [x] Scorecard template finalized and linked in repo.
- [x] KPI data owners confirmed for each metric.
- [ ] First monthly review completed with documented actions.
